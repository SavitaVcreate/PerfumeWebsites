WHO WE ARE

Born from the Heart
of Arabia

In 1987, Abdullah Al-Rashidi — a merchant who travelled the spice routes from Oman to Morocco — returned to Riyadh with a singular vision: to create perfumes that held the spirit of a place, not merely its scent.

He opened a small atelier in Al-Malaz district with three clay vessels of oud, a mortar of amber resin and knowledge passed through generations.

Today, BU Abdullah remains a family house. Every bottle is filled by hand and every accord is authored in-house.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Three Principles.
No Compromise.

I

Provenance

Every raw material carries a passport. Our oud is sourced from Assam and Kalimantan. Our rose absolute from the Taif valley. We pay premium prices to ensure ethical harvest and generational partnerships.

II

Patience

We do not accelerate maturation. Our aged accords rest for a minimum of eighteen months before the first assessment. Time cannot be replaced by technique.

III

Permanence

Our formulas are preserved in handwritten ledgers and entrusted to the next generation. No accord is discontinued. Every scent created will remain available.
