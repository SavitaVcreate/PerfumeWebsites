We process orders as soon as they are received, usually within 0–3 business days. We will contact you in case of any discrepancies or issues during the processing of your order.

Shipping & Delivery

Backorders & Unavailable Products

We do not ship backorders. If an item becomes unavailable after your order has been confirmed, we will inform you within 2 business days.

You will receive a full refund for the unavailable item.

Shipping Address

Each order can be shipped to one shipping address only. If you want products delivered to different addresses, please place separate orders.

Delivery Attempts

We will attempt delivery up to 3 times to the shipping address provided by you.

If we are unable to reach the customer after repeated delivery attempts, we reserve the right to cancel the order.
