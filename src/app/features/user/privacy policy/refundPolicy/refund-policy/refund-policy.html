Return Policy

  • Any queries or concerns related to deliveries should be addressed to the support team within 24 hours of the product being received by the customer.
  • Products can be returned within 15 days from the date of delivery in case of wrong products.
  • Prepaid orders are not eligible for refunds if returned, as cosmetic products are not reusable due to hygiene factors. They will only be re-shipped after sending a query to our support team.
  • Payment refunds will be applicable only on prepaid orders in case incorrect products are delivered.
  • COD orders are not eligible for a refund.
  • BU Abdullah Perfumes will not charge for replacements if wrong products, incomplete sets, incorrect styles, wrong products, or damaged products are received by the customer.
  • It is mandatory to send photos of the invoice and product to proceed with the return or replacement process in case a customer receives damaged products.

Exchanges

Only size exchanges are allowed. Items can be exchanged for a similar size or a different size if the wrong product is delivered.

Return Intimation

You can email us within 15 days from the date of delivery.

The following details should be shared with Customer Support by email:

  • Order Number
  • Courier Docket Number or AWB Number
  • Reason for Return

Guidelines for a Valid Return

  • The product, if any, should be in its original condition.
  • The original box should be retained and returned.

All products sold on BU Abdullah Perfumes are 100% genuine. We understand how much you value every purchase. We are committed to making your shopping experience a delightful one.

Cancellation Policy

An easy cancellation process is available for all our customers. You can cancel your order before the product has been shipped.

Your entire order amount will be refunded in case of online payment, subject to the applicable cancellation conditions.

Refund Policy

  • Refunds are provided forPREPAID orders only, subject to the applicable return and refund conditions.
  • Your refund will be initiated within the applicable working days after receiving and authorizing the refund request.
  • Your refund is estimated to be credited to the account used for the order payment within 4–8 days after initiating the refund.
  • Please note that the actual time required for the refund to appear in your account depends on external agencies such as banks and payment gateways.

Mode of Refund

For both cancelled orders and returned products due to incomplete sets, incorrect styles, wrong sizes, or defective products delivered, the refund amount will be credited back to the same payment method used to make the purchase.
